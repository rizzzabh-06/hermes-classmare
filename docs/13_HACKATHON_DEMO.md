# Hackathon Demo Script

## Opening

"Teachers already know the marks. What they need is to understand why students are struggling and what to teach next."

## Demo Sequence

### 1. Open Dashboard

Show:

- Grade 8 Mathematics,
- recent assessment,
- weak concept alert,
- pending weekly plan.

### 2. Voice Assessment Creation

Use Wispr Flow:

"Create a three-question Grade 8 assessment on quadrilateral angle properties."

Show the transcript becoming a structured assessment draft.

### 3. Evaluate Subjective Answer

Open a seeded student response.

Trigger evaluation.

Explain:

- Hermes orchestrates the workflow.
- OpenAI powers Hermes as the underlying reasoning provider.
- The app does not use a parallel direct OpenAI integration.

Show:

- marks,
- rubric points,
- misconception,
- confidence,
- review flag.

### 4. Approve Result

Edit or approve feedback.

Show Convex updating the dashboard live.

### 5. Class Intelligence

Open heatmap.

Show:

- weak concept,
- common misconception,
- students needing support.

### 6. Generate Weekly Plan

Trigger planner.

Show Linkup-enriched resource.

Explain:

- static curriculum remains source of truth,
- Linkup enriches but does not define curriculum.

### 7. Spoken Explanation

Click `Explain this concept`.

Preview text.

Play ElevenLabs audio.

### 8. Deployment and Commercialization

Mention:

- frontend on Cloudflare Pages,
- APIs on Cloudflare Workers,
- Dodo Payments planned for school subscriptions.

## Closing

"This is not another dashboard. It is a teaching decision engine built around the actual evidence inside student answers."
